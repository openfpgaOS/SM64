What this identifier marks
--------------------------

Files under src/sm64/sm64/ that are derived from Nintendo's Super Mario 64
(Nintendo 64, 1996) by decompilation, disassembly, or data extraction. This
includes, but is not limited to:

  * the decompiled game C sources and headers (src/, actors/, levels/,
    include/ other than include/PR/, data/, text/, assets/)
  * hand-written and generated assembly derived from the retail ROM (asm/)
  * data tables describing the contents and byte offsets of the retail ROM
    (assets.json, assets/demo_data.json)
  * the sound bank and sequence descriptions (sound/sequences.json,
    sound/sound_banks/*.json, sound/*), which describe Nintendo's audio data
  * any other file whose content is a transcription of, or a description of
    the internal layout of, the retail ROM
