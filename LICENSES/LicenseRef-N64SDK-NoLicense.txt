What this identifier marks
--------------------------

Nintendo 64 SDK material vendored under src/sm64/sm64/, namely:

  * include/PR/**            libultra / Fast3D public headers
  * lib/asm/**, lib/src/**   libultra (the N64 operating system library),
                             as decompiled/reconstructed by the decomp project
  * tools/sdk-tools/**       a decompilation of Nintendo's N64 SDK developer
                             tools (adpcm, tabledesign); see its README.md
  * lib/PR/**                RSP microcode BINARIES (24 .bin files)     [1]
  * lib/rsp.s, rsp/**        RSP microcode sources / build glue         [1]

  [1] These paths were removed from this repository's git index while this
      notice was being written (the working-tree copies were deleted and the
      deletions staged). The rules covering them are retained in REUSE.toml so
      that they remain correctly classified in history and if the paths ever
      return. If the paths are absent in your checkout, the corresponding
      rules simply match nothing.

Evidence of provenance (verified in-tree, not assumed)
------------------------------------------------------

include/PR/gbi.h and its siblings carry this header verbatim:

    Copyright (C) 1994, Silicon Graphics, Inc.

    These coded instructions, statements, and computer programs contain
    unpublished proprietary information of Silicon Graphics, Inc., and
    are protected by Federal copyright law. They may not be disclosed
    to third parties or copied or duplicated in any form, in whole or
    in part, without the prior written consent of Silicon Graphics, Inc.

That is an explicit reservation of rights and an explicit prohibition on
copying. It is the opposite of a license grant.

The microcode binaries under lib/PR/ contained embedded Nintendo authorship
strings; the following were read directly out of the tracked files before they
were removed:

    lib/PR/f3dex/F3DEX_NoN_data.bin
        "RSP Gfx ucode F3DEX.NoN     1.23 Yoshitaka Yasumoto Nintendo."
    lib/PR/f3dex2/F3DEX2_data.bin
        "RSP Gfx ucode F3DEX       fifo 2.08  Yoshitaka Yasumoto 1999 Nintendo."
    lib/PR/s2dex/S2DEX_data.bin
        "RSP Gfx ucode S2DEX  1.07 Yoshitaka Yasumoto Nintendo."

These are unmodified Nintendo binaries, not clean-room reimplementations.
