What this identifier marks
--------------------------

Original (not Nintendo-derived) work authored by the contributors to the
upstream decompilation and its PC/DOS port chain, vendored under
src/sm64/sm64/. Typical examples:

  * src/pc/** other than src/pc/gfx/** -- the PC port shim: configfile.c,
    audio backends, controller backends, mixer.c, pc_main.c,
    ultra_reimplementation.c
  * tools/*.c, tools/*.h, tools/*.cpp and tools/*.py written by the decomp
    project (asm_processor/, textconv.c, hashtable.c, aifc_decode.c,
    aiff_extract_codebook.c, extract_data_for_mio.c, patch_libultra_math.c,
    gen_asset_list.cpp, determine-endian-bitwidth.c, the *.py helpers, the
    *.sh helpers)
  * project scaffolding: Makefile, Makefile.split, sm64.ld, Dockerfile,
    Jenkinsfile, Doxyfile, diff.py, format.sh, README.md, CHANGES,
    .clang-format, .clang-tidy, .gitattributes, .gitignore, charmap*.txt,
    undefined_syms.txt, *.sha1

Files in these areas that carry their own explicit license notice are NOT
covered by this identifier; they are annotated separately in REUSE.toml (see
tools/sm64tools.LICENSE, tools/n64graphics_ci_dir/LICENSE, the MIT notice at
the top of tools/armips.cpp, the LGPL notice at the top of
tools/audiofile/audiofile.cpp, tools/stb/*, tools/utf8.c,
tools/ido5.3_compiler/LICENSE.md, src/pc/gfx/LICENSE.txt).

Upstream identity
-----------------

Verified from in-tree evidence:

  * src/sm64/sm64/README.md is titled "Super Mario 64 DOS Port" and embeds
    images from raw.githubusercontent.com/fgsfdsfgs/sm64-port/dos/media/...,
    identifying the immediate upstream as fgsfdsfgs/sm64-port, branch "dos".
  * Makefile sets TARGET_DOS ?= 1 and COMPILER ?= ido, and selects an
    i586-pc-msdosdjgpp-* toolchain; DOS-only sources are present
    (src/pc/gfx/gfx_dos_api.c, src/pc/audio/audio_sb16.c,
    lib/{allegro,dmesa,glide3,osmesa}).
  * The first line of CHANGES is "Refresh 11", identifying the decompilation
    base as n64decomp/sm64 Refresh 11.

The exact upstream commit SHA is NOT recoverable from this repository: the
vendored tree is a flattened copy with no .gitmodules and no nested .git.
No SHA is asserted here rather than inventing one.
